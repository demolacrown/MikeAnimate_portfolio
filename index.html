THE CREATOR'S VISION

Made with imagination. Rooted in heritage.

MIKEANIMATE is an independent creative project dedicated to reimagining African history through animation and digital storytelling.

The vision is to create memorable historical films featuring African kingdoms, legendary characters, cultural traditions and events that deserve to be explored by a global audience.

Through research, character design, cinematic environments and animation, MIKEANIMATE aims to make history engaging while respecting the people and cultures behind each story.
